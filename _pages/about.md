# Junteng Liu

I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, where I am a member of the **HKUST NLP Group** advised by **Prof. Junxian He**. My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning (ML)**.

I completed my **B.Eng. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** from 2020 to 2024 and graduated in June 2024. During my undergraduate studies, I was also advised by Prof. Junxian He.

## Skills & Research Interests

* Natural language processing (NLP)
* Machine learning (ML)
* LLM reasoning
* Reinforcement learning (RL)
* Hallucination in vision-language models (VLM)
* LLM truthfulness
* Interpretability
